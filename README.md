# 🖥️ open-posture - Improve your posture with your camera

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Open_Posture-blue.svg)](https://github.com/valuable-familygerreidae648/open-posture)

open-posture keeps you sitting straight while you work at your computer. It uses the camera built into your machine to check your posture in real time. The software processes all video data on your local hardware. It does not send images to the internet or save your personal data. 

## 🛠️ Requirements

You need a computer running Windows 10 or Windows 11. Your computer must have a functional webcam. The software works best with a clear view of your head and shoulders. Ensure your work area has enough light so the camera can see you.

## ⚙️ Initial Setup

After the installation finishes, find the open-posture icon on your desktop or in your start menu. Double-click the icon to launch the program. 

The first time the program starts, it asks for permission to use your camera. You must grant this permission for the posture coach to work. Select the camera you want to use from the drop-down menu if you have more than one device connected.

## 📈 Using the Coach

The main dashboard shows a live preview of your posture. A green indicator means your posture is good. A yellow or red indicator shows that you need to adjust your seating position.

You can change how sensitive the alerts are in the settings menu. Higher sensitivity gives you more frequent feedback. Lower sensitivity allows for more movement before an alert sounds. 

## 🛡️ Privacy and Safety

This software does not use any cloud servers. All calculations happen inside the application on your computer. Nothing leaves your machine. Your video stream stays private and disappears from the short memory cache as soon as you close the application. 

## 🧩 Troubleshooting Common Issues

If the camera does not show an image:
- Check if another application uses your camera. Close other video apps like Zoom or Teams.
- Ensure your camera privacy cover is open.
- Restart the application.

If the posture detection is inaccurate:
- Move your chair so you sit centered in the frame.
- Adjust your desk lamp to remove shadows from your face.
- Clean your camera lens with a soft cloth.

## 📝 Frequently Asked Questions

Does this software store my video? 
No. The software only looks at your posture. It discards every frame after it processes the visual data.

Can I run this on a laptop?
Yes. It works with most internal laptop webcams.

Does it require an internet connection?
No. The application runs entirely offline. You do not need to be online to keep your posture in check.

How does it detect posture?
The app uses MediaPipe to map points on your body. It calculates the angle of your neck and spine based on these points. It compares your current position to a healthy baseline.
